Privacy Policy

Sunday Roast Companion

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Overview

Sunday Roast Companion is designed to help you plan and manage the timing of a Sunday roast. The app does not require you to create an account.

Information stored by the app

The app stores information needed to create and manage your roast plan, such as your selected roast, cooking options, serving time, task progress and completed tasks. This information is stored locally on your device so that an active roast can be resumed.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Sunday Roast Companion uses local notifications to remind you when cooking tasks are due to begin. Notification permission can be changed at any time in your iPhone Settings.

Personal information and tracking

The current version of Sunday Roast Companion does not require an account, does not use advertising, and is not designed to track you across apps or websites.
